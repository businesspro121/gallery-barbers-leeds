About the shop

A chair for everyone, in the middle of Leeds.

Gallery Barbers sits on Stall 10–11 inside Leeds Kirkgate Market, one of the great covered markets in the country. We cut in the middle of it all — between the stalls, the noise and the daily rhythm of the city.

We’re a unisex shop, and we mean it. A skin fade and a line-up, a beard shaped and finished with a hot towel, a long restyle with colour through it, a child’s first haircut — all of it happens here, in the same room, on the same day.

The work is straightforward and done properly: a clean line, a fade that blends, a shape that suits the person in the chair. You’re welcome to just turn up — we’ve always been a walk-in shop. If you’d rather know your slot is waiting, send us a request below or a quick message on WhatsApp.

  • Unisex — everyone welcome Men, women and children. Barbering, ladies’ cutting, colour and styling under one roof.
  • Inside Kirkgate Market Stall 10–11, a couple of minutes’ walk from Leeds bus station and the city centre.
  • Walk in or book ahead No appointment necessary. Booking simply saves you the wait when the market’s busy.
