About the shop

A barber’s chair in the middle of Leeds.

Gallery Barbers sits on Stall 10–11 inside Leeds Kirkgate Market, one of the great covered markets in the country. We cut in the middle of it all — between the stalls, the noise and the daily rhythm of the city.

The work is straightforward and done properly: a clean line, a fade that blends, a beard shaped to suit the face in front of us. We cut for men, women and children, and we take the same care whether it’s a first haircut or a fortnightly tidy-up.

You’re welcome to just turn up — we’ve always been a walk-in shop. If you’d rather know your slot is waiting, send us a request below or a quick message on WhatsApp.

  • Inside Kirkgate Market Stall 10–11, a couple of minutes’ walk from Leeds bus station and the city centre.
  • Cuts for everyone Men, women and children — from skin fades to long-hair styling and colour.
  • Walk in or book ahead No appointment necessary. Booking simply saves you the wait at busy times.
